HER LITTLE BOOK - MADE WITH LOVE FROM TRAFORD

Windows: extract the whole ZIP folder, then double-click:

    Open Her Little Book.bat

The diary works without installing Python or Ollama. The small minimized
server window must stay open while the book is being used. Close it to stop
the local site.

On the first visit, choose a name and password. The diary is saved only in
that browser on that computer; this ZIP does not include Traford's diary.
Use the profile button inside the diary to download an encrypted backup.
Keep the backup and password somewhere private.

Ollama chat is optional and needs Python plus Ollama installed. All the diary's
pages, ideas, calendar, reminders, and photos work without AI.
